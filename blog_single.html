Why most ERP projects run over budget

By Farhana Rahman, Principal Consultant

After 40 ERP implementations, the same three causes explain nearly every overrun. None of them are technical.

1. Scope is agreed before processes are mapped

Teams sign a fixed scope, then discover that each department runs the same process differently. Map the real workflows first, then price the work.

2. Data migration is treated as a final step

Dirty product and customer records surface late and block testing. Start cleaning data in the first month, and assign an owner in each department.

3. Nobody owns adoption

Training is scheduled for the week before launch. Name a champion per team early and let them test every release.
