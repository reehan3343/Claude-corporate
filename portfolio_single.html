One system for stock, billing and reporting, rolled out across 60 stores in nine months without a single trading day lost.

The challenge

Sixty stores, sixty spreadsheets

Each store tracked stock in its own spreadsheet. Head office could not see what was selling or where stock was running out, so replenishment was guesswork and month-end took almost two weeks.

Our approach

Map first, then build

We mapped how stores really worked, designed one ERP covering inventory, billing and reporting, and rolled it out in three waves so no store went live without tested data.

ERP corePOS integrationDemand forecastingLive dashboardsSingle sign-onStaff training

Process

Three phases, nine months

  1. 01

    Discover and map

    Store visits, workflow mapping and a single data model agreed with every department.

    Months 1 to 2
  2. 02

    Build and migrate

  3. 03
