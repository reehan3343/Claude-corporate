About us

A small team that stays on the project

Northline started in 2014 with one belief: advice is only useful if someone is accountable for the result. Today 40 consultants, designers and engineers work from Dhaka and Singapore.

You get the same people from the first workshop to the final handover. No handoffs to a junior team after the contract is signed.
